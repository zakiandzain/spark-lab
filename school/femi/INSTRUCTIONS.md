## Contemporary First Nations, Métis, and Inuit Voices Presentation

In this project, you will work in groups of two to create and deliver an **oral presentation (90-120 seconds) with a visual component** about a contemporary artist, athlete, cultural tradition, or any other aspect of Indigeneity that interests you. The primary goal is to center, value, and respect contemporary First Nations, Métis, and Inuit voices.

A key part of this project is to apply the protocol rules developed in class to **how** you conduct your research. This ethical engagement is integral to evaluation.

### Step 1: Choose Your Topic

1. **Identify Your Interests:** Individually, reflect on personal interests (artistic, athletic, social, political, etc.) and select a few areas to explore.
2. **Find Authentic Voices:** Search for contemporary First Nations, Inuit, and Métis voices connected to those areas. Start with the Indigenous news sources listed below.
3. **Apply Protocol Rules:** Be guided by class protocol rules, including **positionality, reciprocity, and respect**. Research cannot simply be taking knowledge.
4. **Record Sources:** Record sources for an **annotated bibliography** (minimum **5 sources**). Each source needs a short explanation (3–4 sentences) of how you applied one or more protocol rules.

### Step 2: Research and Create Your Presentation

1. **Conduct Research:** Gather primary and secondary Indigenous knowledge sources (oral teachings, interviews, art, articles by Indigenous authors, etc.).
2. **Write Your Script:** With your partner, write a 90–120 second script: introduction, engaging information, conclusion.
3. **Create a Visual Component:** Required visual aid — slideshow with images, embedded video, or other audiovisuals that enhance the topic.
4. **Practice Your Delivery:** Rehearse for the time limit. Eye contact, pacing, and clear vocabulary are evaluated.

### Step 3: Present and Reflect

1. **Deliver Your Presentation** to the class with your partner.
2. **Submit an Annotated Bibliography** (min. 5 sources, [MLA format](https://www.mybib.com/tools/mla-citation-generator)) with insightful explanations of how protocol rules guided research.
