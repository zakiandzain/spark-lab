**Languages and Oral Traditions**
- Hundreds of Indigenous languages exist across North America
- Stories and oral traditions pass knowledge between generations
- Language plays a key role in cultural identity

## Slide 5 — Community Life
Community and family relationships are central in many Native American cultures, supporting cooperation, respect, and shared responsibilities.

Daily life has traditionally been shaped by environment, traditions, and social values.

## Slide 6 — Artistic Expression

## Slide 7 — Spiritual Beliefs
**Spirituality and Values**

Spiritual beliefs vary widely among Native American communities and often emphasize respect for nature, balance, and harmony.

These beliefs are deeply connected to cultural traditions and daily life.

## Slide 8 — History and Resilience
Native American communities have faced many historical challenges while continuing to preserve their cultures and identities.

Their resilience remains an important part of their story today.
