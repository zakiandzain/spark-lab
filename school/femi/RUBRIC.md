## B3: Speaking Skills and Strategies

| Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 | Level 4 |
|---------|---------|---------|---------|
| Eye contact may be weak (looking at screen or paper too much). Little expression. Limited vocabulary / fillers interfere. Pacing too fast or slow. Limited voice projection. Practice barely noticeable. | Eye contact satisfactory (looking at screen/paper at times). Some expression. Satisfactory vocabulary (fillers used). Satisfactory pacing. Satisfactory projection.
